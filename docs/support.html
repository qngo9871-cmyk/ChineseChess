FAQ

How do I play?
Tap a piece to select it, then tap a highlighted square to move. Red moves first.

What are the difficulty levels?
Beginner, Medium, and Expert control how deeply the AI thinks ahead.

Can I play with a friend?
Yes — choose "Play vs Friend" for pass-and-play on the same device.
